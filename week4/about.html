About

Learn more about the campus notice board.

The University of Luzon Notice Board is a simple place for students, faculty, and staff to share and read campus announcements.

Instead of checking many different places, everyone can find the latest updates here, from academic reminders to campus events.

Our goal is to keep communication across the campus clear, organized, and easy to access.

Our Purpose

Make campus communication clear and easy for everyone.

Who Can Use It

Students, faculty, and staff of the university.

What You Can Find

Academic reminders, events, and general announcements.
